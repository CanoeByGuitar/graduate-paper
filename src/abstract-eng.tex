\chapter*{\zihao{-3}\heiti{ABSTRACT}}

\vspace{-0.5cm}


\setlength{\baselineskip}{17pt}
With the continuous improvement of computer hardware performance and the enhancement of graphics processing capabilities, graphics simulation has been widely applied in many fields such as game development and film production. High-precision interactive simulation between fluids and elastoplastic solids has become a research hotspot. The Material Point Method (MPM) is a simulation method that can achieve the mutual coupling of various materials such as fluids, elastomers, and rigid bodies, and has achieved remarkable results in many application fields. However, it still has inherent problems at the algorithm level. For example, the Material Point Method will generate numerical dissipation when simulating fluid-solid coupling, and non-physical voids will appear at the collision boundaries. On the other hand, the demand for the time efficiency of physical simulation solutions in the film, television, and game industries is increasing day by day. Therefore, GPU optimization algorithms are becoming increasingly important in physical simulations. In response to the above problems, based on the Material Point Method, this paper proposes a set of fluid-solid coupling simulation methods that integrate adaptive meshing and support discontinuous velocity fields, and further presents a GPU optimization scheme.

In terms of the algorithm, aiming at the limitation of numerical viscosity of the traditional Material Point Method on the unified background mesh, this paper modifies the solution process of the Material Point Method, introduces the Decomposition Compatibility Affine Particle-In-Cell (DC-APIC) integrator, enforces the free-slip boundary condition in the tangential direction, realizes the separation condition in the normal direction, and designs a segmentation method based on the phase field gradient. The effectiveness of this method has been verified through various simulations of the interaction between complex fluids and elastoplastic solids. Aiming at the problem of numerical instability of the DC-APIC integrator at the sharp edges of solids, and the phenomenon of non-physical voids at the collision boundaries of objects processed by the traditional Material Point Method, this paper proposes an adaptive mesh algorithm that integrates the DC-APIC integrator. To meet the demand for dynamic mesh division under fluid-solid coupling conditions, this paper uses the particle hierarchy based on the phase field gradient to drive the mesh division. Experiments show that this method effectively preserves the collision details at different scales without adding excessive additional overhead, and can achieve high-precision fluid-solid interface simulation. In terms of parallel optimization, based on the GPU-MPM algorithm framework, this paper proposes a GPU acceleration scheme for discontinuous velocity fields, and designs a warp write optimization algorithm for discontinuous data. In the simulation test of high-precision and large-scale scenarios, the computational efficiency of the GPU optimization algorithm in this paper is 17.7 to 25.7 times that of the CPU-side Material Point Method accelerated by SIMD.

By integrating the DC-APIC particle mesh transfer method, the adaptive Material Point Method, and the GPU optimization strategy, the fluid-solid coupling simulation framework in this paper can efficiently simulate the interaction scenarios between common fluids and elastoplastic solids, and retain high-resolution effects and the physical correctness of the natural separation of the fluid-solid two phases at the fluid-solid interface. At the same time, the framework in this paper only relies on a single background mesh, does not require complex geometric boundary constraints, is simple to implement, and is suitable for the special effects production of large-scale fluid-solid scene simulations.
